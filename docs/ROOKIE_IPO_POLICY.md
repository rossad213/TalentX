# TalentX Rookie IPO Policy

## Purpose

A drafted rookie does not have enough professional performance history for the normal Active Career Model. TalentX therefore opens the listing with a transparent Rookie IPO model.

## Opening score

- 35% draft capital
- 20% pre-professional performance
- 15% expected immediate opportunity
- 10% position value
- 8% age and development potential
- 7% availability and injury outlook
- 5% audience demand

The opening price is a category-specific base value plus the weighted score multiplied by a league price scale. All prototype values are simulated. Production scores require timestamped, licensed or otherwise permitted data sources.

## Draft-weight decay

Draft reputation must not permanently protect a player. The target schedule is:

| Stage | Draft-capital weight |
|---|---:|
| Rookie IPO | 35% |
| Start of first professional season | 25% |
| Mid-rookie season | 15% |
| End of first season | 8% |
| After second season | 0–3% |

The removed weight transfers primarily to observed professional performance, role stability, availability, and career momentum.

## Position value

Position is a market input, not a judgment of real-world importance. It reflects how much measurable performance, award upside, audience demand, and market liquidity the position typically generates inside TalentX. Position cannot override draft capital or demonstrated performance by itself.

## Listing lifecycle

`Prospect → Pre-Draft → Drafted → Rookie IPO → Active Rookie → Early Career → Established → Veteran → Retired — Legacy`

Profiles must carry a `career_stage`, source record, verification timestamp, and model version.
